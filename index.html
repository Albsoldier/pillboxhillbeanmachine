Our history

A corner, a second-hand machine, and a stubborn idea.

Pillbox Hill Bean Machine started life as a window in a wall on the corner of the block — one hand-me-down espresso machine, two crates of cups, and a queue that was mostly taxi drivers, because they were the only people awake before seven.

The machine was loud, the counter was a door on trestles, and the whole thing ran on the belief that a good cup of coffee shouldn't cost you your lunch break. We still hold to that. Nothing on the menu takes longer to make than it takes to explain.

Today the counter is real wood, the grinder actually matches the machine, and you can order from your phone before you've found your keys. Same beans. Same corner. A lot less shouting over steam.
